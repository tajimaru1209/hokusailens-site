How to Use

  1. Pick a view. The list shows all 46 views, nearest first, with a map, the direction of Mount Fuji, and walking directions.
  2. Go there, or find the same shape. Some places are known to the street; for others only the area is known. For views with no fixed spot, find the same shape anywhere — a mitate.
  3. Line it up. The print floats over the camera as a faded image or as outlines. Pinch to zoom, move and scale the print to fit the scene, and follow the arrow to face Fuji.
  4. Share then and now. Swipe between the print and your photo, and share a side-by-side image or a short wipe video.
  5. Fill the album. Each view you photograph gets a seal: 現 on site, 見 as a mitate.

FAQ

Where do the print images come from?
All 46 prints are from The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York, released as public domain (CC0) through its Open Access program.

Are the locations exact?
No. Some places Hokusai drew are well known, others are debated, and he often changed what he saw. The app marks each view as “Spot known”, “Area known” or “Anywhere”. If you know a better spot, please tell us.

Why doesn’t my photo match perfectly?
Hokusai made Fuji bigger and moved things around, and cities have changed in almost 200 years. Match the main shapes — the slope, the bridge, the line of the horizon — not every detail.

What is free?
Seven views are open from the start. One in-app purchase opens all 46 for good. Use Restore Purchase on a new device.

What happens if I delete the app?
Your photos are stored only inside the app, so they are deleted with it. Save the images you want to keep to Photos first.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Hokusai Lens does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Camera and photos

Photos you take are saved only inside the app on your device. They leave it only when you share them yourself.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location and compass on your device to sort views by distance, to show the direction of Mount Fuji, and to mark a photo as taken on site. Your location is never sent anywhere.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Analytics and ads

The app contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes
